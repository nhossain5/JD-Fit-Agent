The ICE Internship Program offers a dynamic opportunity to combine hands-on, impactful work on projects that shape our business, alongside practical career and professional development. You'll build meaningful connections, strengthen your interpersonal skills, and contribute in ways that truly make a difference. 

 

Beyond your day-to-day responsibilities, you'll participate in weekly sessions led by industry leaders, connect with fellow interns, and deepen your understanding of the financial services, technology, and mortgage industries. 

The Software Development Engineer in Test Intern will support core digital asset infrastructure projects, gaining exposure to blockchain technologies and test automation. 

Responsibilities 

 

Design and develop tools to migrate code from a legacy automation framework to the new tech stack 
Build an understanding of blockchain technologies and develop automation scripts to test core workflows for adding new digital assets 
Learn how to build test scenarios and test cases 
Find and log defects discovered during the testing process 
Perform additional duties and contribute to projects as assigned 
 

Knowledge and Experience 

 

Must be currently enrolled in a degree program at an accredited university and be at least advancing to their junior year of study 
JavaScript/TypeScript experience 
Basic SQL knowledge 
Understanding of blockchain and crypto markets is a plus 
 

Internship Program Schedule 

 

To accommodate varying academic calendars, the ICE Internship Program offers two schedule options: 

Option 1: May 24, 2027 – August 6, 2027 
Option 2: June 7, 2027 – August 20, 2027 
 

Candidates will be asked to select their preferred session during the application process. 

 

Please Note: Interns must be available to work full-time, five days a week, at our Atlanta office for the entire duration of one of the designated program schedules. Partial participation or alternate dates cannot be accommodated.
